Water Safety Is a Life Skill, Not a Luxury

Drowning is the #1 cause of accidental death for young children in Florida. Our survival-swim method gives your child the skills to stay calm and safe in the water, while building a love for it that lasts a lifetime.

  • Float, roll, and breathe taught step by step, so your child knows what to do if they ever fall in.
  • Warm, heated pools in Plantation, or your coach comes to your home pool across Broward County.
  • Gentle, never rushed. We build trust first, so even anxious little ones learn to love the water.
