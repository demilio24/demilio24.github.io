Schedule & Sessions

How class times and sessions actually work.

Both programs meet weekly during the school year, right after the final bell on your campus. What changes from program to program (and sometimes school to school) is the session length, so here's the breakdown before you register.

Program 1

Martial Arts

Authentic Systema, scaled for kids. Weekly on-campus classes that build discipline, focus, and real self-defense skill.

Days Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri
Time Right after dismissal
Length 30 to 60 min
Ages Pre-K to 8th
CadenceMost campuses run month-to-month, with new families welcome to start any time.
QuarterlyA few partner schools run on a quarterly session (look for "quarterly" on the campus card below).
TestingsBelt testings happen every quarter so kids always have a clear next goal.
  • Classes grouped by grade band (PreK to K, 1st to 5th, etc.) so kids train with peers.
  • You'll get a welcome email with your campus calendar and start date the same week you register.
  • Pause or cancel any month, no long contracts.
Register, Martial Arts
Program 2

Spirit Dance

Pom, hip-hop, and team-spirit choreography. Weekly practices that build confidence, coordination, and a big-stage end-of-session showcase.

Days Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri
Time Right after dismissal
Length 30 to 45 min
Ages Pre-K to 6th
CadenceMost Spirit Dance squads run on a quarterly session so we can build a routine and end with a showcase.
MonthlyA few campuses also offer a month-to-month option for younger dancers, perfect for a trial run.
ShowcaseEvery session wraps with a parent-friendly performance, dates shared in your welcome email.
  • Mixed-age squads grouped by grade band so the choreography fits the dancers.
  • No prior dance experience needed, we coach from day one.
  • Costume and showcase details go out at least 3 weeks before the performance.
Register, Spirit Dance
