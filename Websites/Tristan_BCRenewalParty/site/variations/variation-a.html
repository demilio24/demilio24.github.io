A New Provincial Movement

A New Future for British Columbia.

British Columbia has the land, the talent, and the resources to be one of the best places in the world to live. We have simply been governed without ambition. The BC Renewal Party is building a province powered by clean energy, where every resident shares in our resource wealth, housing is affordable, and young families can afford to stay.

Why We're Running

01 / The Case for Renewal
A Province Off Course

For too long, politics in British Columbia has been about protecting the status quo, not protecting people. Housing costs have pushed families out of their communities. Healthcare waitlists have left loved ones in pain. Wages have not kept up with the cost of living.

A lot of British Columbians feel politically homeless, tired of the same old back-and-forth that got us here. We are running because change only comes when everyday people step forward. We are not here to play politics. We are here to build solutions that work.

We're Builders

02 / Who We Are
Leadership · Experience · Values

We're not politicians. We're builders.

The BC Renewal Party was founded by everyday people who are tired of waiting for the system to fix itself: workers, parents, small business owners, and entrepreneurs.

We believe BC can be bold again, not by clinging to old ideologies, but by investing in real solutions that help people live, work, and thrive. We are creating a genuine alternative.

Meet Tristan Tolley.

03 / Our Founder
In His Own Words

Our founder, Tristan Tolley, is a 25-year-old entrepreneur and caregiver who stepped up to raise his younger brothers after losing his father. He runs a private swim-lessons business and has his first child on the way.

He has lived the failures of the current system: education cuts growing up, a healthcare system that makes families wait, and housing speculation that put homeownership out of reach for his generation.

"I never planned to start a political party. But I looked around and saw a province where young people cannot afford to live, work, or dream. If we do not step up, no one will. This movement is not about power. It is about possibility, for every person who has been overlooked, overtaxed, or underestimated."

Tristan TolleyFounder, BC Renewal Party

Our Vision

Bold Ideas. Real Change. A Better BC.

04 / Six Pillars

We believe in a BC that works for everyone, not just the few at the top: people before politics, solutions before ideology, and future generations before election cycles.

Pillar 01

Economic security to live with dignity.

Pillar 02

A protected environment with modern, affordable clean energy.

Pillar 03

Healthcare that is accessible, efficient, and compassionate.

Pillar 04

Housing that is affordable for families, workers, and young people.

Pillar 05

Education that prepares the next generation to build and innovate.

Pillar 06

Resources that benefit all British Columbians, not just corporations.

Our Philosophy

Seven Core Values

05 / What Guides Us

Pragmatic progressivism with an economically libertarian flair: progressive in our goals, but free, simple, and common-sense in our methods. Results over rhetoric.

1

Innovation & Growth

Bold ideas like nuclear energy, manufacturing, hemp, and tech-driven prosperity.

2

Economic Security for All

Every British Columbian deserves a life of dignity.

3

Self-Reliance & Opportunity

Reduce barriers, reward hard work, a fair shot for everyone.

4

Freedom & Transparency

Cut red tape, protect civil liberties, radical openness.

5

Generational Renewal

Reverse the youth exodus; a province the next generation wants to stay in.

6

Unity & Shared Prosperity

Reject divisive politics; shared opportunity for all.

7

Competence & Accountability

Fiscal responsibility and a government that delivers.
