Ages 3 months – 2.5 years

Fearless Floaters Level 1

Fearless Floater Cadets are brand new to the pool — or maybe just nervous about it — and this is where their hero journey begins. With their coach right by their side, your cadet will get comfy floating on their back, putting their face in the water, holding onto the wall like a pro, and practicing safe falls into the pool. A few tears are totally normal here — they’re part of the Superhero Swim Process!

Kowabunga Kickers Level 2

KowaBunga Kicker Cadets love the water — maybe a little too much! They’ve got the confidence but not yet the safety skills to match, so this level is all about the superpowers that keep them safe. Your cadet will learn to kick their way to the nearest wall, climb out on their own, and turn a surprise jump into a calm, safe trip back to the edge.

Puppy Paddlers Level 3

Puppy Paddler Cadets have the safety basics down — they can jump in, catch a breath, and find their way to the wall. Now it’s time to actually swim! Your cadet will learn real arm strokes, how to roll onto their back for a breath, and how to power all the way across the pool on their own. (Get the camera ready.)

Power Stroke Level 4

Power Stroke Cadets can already swim — now it’s time to swim smart! These cadets are confident and can cover real distance, but they’re ready to fine-tune the details. In this class they’ll master proper side breathing, smooth out their stroke, and learn to swim longer without getting tired. Perfect for ages 4–6 who are ready to take their swimming to the next level.
