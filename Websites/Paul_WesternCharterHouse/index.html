Our Purpose

Mission

Western Charter House exists to identify British Columbia’s most pressing challenges, understand them without bias and develop practical, evidence-based solutions that improve the lives of British Columbians.

We believe the best solutions begin with understanding the problem. Through rigorous research, honest public engagement, strategic communications and principled advocacy, we empower citizens to understand the issues, shape better public policy and help build a freer, stronger and more prosperous British Columbia.

Our Principles

Values

People First

We prioritize the lived experiences and voices of everyday British Columbians across all 93 ridings. We listen actively and measure success by real improvements in their daily lives.

Principled Pragmatism

We champion practical solutions rooted in free enterprise, fiscal responsibility, individual liberty, social continuity, and accountable governance, always focused on delivering optimal outcomes for constituents.

Non-Partisan Integrity

We operate proudly independent of any political party. We pursue honest analysis and the best results for British Columbians, regardless of political affiliation.

Rigorous Honesty

We demand intellectual honesty and rigorous scrutiny in all our work. We examine who funds secondary research and evidence, verify that studies are methodologically sound, and remain vigilant against work shaped by predetermined outcomes. We prioritize primary data, transparent sources, and measurable real-world results over narrative-driven claims.

Provincial Autonomy & Confederation

We believe British Columbia is a vital part of the Canadian Confederation, with the Charter of Rights and Freedoms as the highest law of the land. At the same time, we strongly affirm the constitutional right of British Columbians to meaningful provincial autonomy, including control over our resources, policies, and future.

Empowered Citizenship

What sets us apart is our inclusive and open process. We recognize that many citizens do not wish to join a political party, and that is their choice. Through Charter House membership, they can still play a meaningful role in solving British Columbia’s problems. We equip members and citizens with clear knowledge, practical tools, and opportunities to understand issues, shape solutions, and drive meaningful change in their communities and beyond.

Long-Term Stewardship

We safeguard and strengthen institutions that have traditionally produced strong outcomes for British Columbians, while courageously addressing and reforming those that no longer deliver results due to bureaucracy, negligence, or misalignment with reality, all for the benefit of current and future generations.
