Common Questions

Everything You Want to Know Before You Reach Out

We get these questions often. Here are honest, straightforward answers.

Do you offer more than survival swim? +
Yes. AquaSquad is now a full-service swim school. Alongside Survival Swim, we offer private learn-to-swim and stroke development, mobile lessons, small group and sibling/friend lessons, and adult lessons, with Parent & Me classes coming soon. Every program is built on survival swim principles: we teach self-rescue first and strokes second.
Do you come to my home? +
Yes. With our Mobile Learn-to-Swim lessons we bring instruction to your home or community pool. Mobile pricing matches our standard lesson packages, and availability depends on your location, so reach out to confirm we can come to you. Note that Survival Swim is taught by Becca at our pool only.
Do you accept Step Up for Students? +
Yes. AquaSquad Swim is an approved Step Up for Students Direct Provider. Eligible families may use scholarship funds toward qualifying programs. Contact us and we'll walk you through how to apply your funds.
Is there a registration fee? +
Yes. A one-time, non-refundable $100 registration fee applies to all programs. After that, you only pay for your lessons, whether weekly, by package, or through a monthly membership.
What if my child cries during lessons? +
Crying is communication, and it's completely normal when your child is experiencing something new. My goal is never to push a child into fear. We move at their pace, build trust first, and celebrate every win. As their confidence grows, crying fades… and pride takes over. Joy always follows confidence.
What should my child wear to lessons? +
If your child isn't potty trained, they must wear a 2-ply system: a reusable swim diaper plus a vinyl or plastic cover. Disposable swim diapers alone don't seal properly. Please bring two towels to every session.
Do kids need refresher sessions? +
Yes! Children grow so quickly, and their changing body composition affects their buoyancy. Skills don't disappear, but growing bodies need updated practice. I recommend refreshers every 4 to 6 months for children under 5, and yearly (or skill-expansion lessons) for children over 5.
Will my child be afraid of the water after lessons? +
Not with AquaSquad's approach. I prioritize emotional safety, sensory-motor readiness, positive reinforcement, trust, and consistency. Children learn that they can do it, and that builds real joy and confidence in the water. My goal is for every child to leave loving the water.
Can my child eat before lessons? +
Please avoid: food for 1 hour before lessons, dairy for 90 to 120 minutes before, and apples, pears, peaches, mango, pineapple, or kiwi for 48 hours before. These foods can cause gas and discomfort, which affects floating and breath control. Comfort = confidence.
Do you allow make-up lessons? +
No. Consistency is the key to this program working. If you need to cancel, I'll reschedule or give you credit. If I ever cancel a session, I'll always make it up.
Can I watch the lessons? +
Yes! You'll watch from the parent area for most of the program. As your child approaches their Level Up milestone, I'll bring you into the water so you feel connected, confident, and ready to safely support your swimmer. We're a team in this.
Can we practice in the pool on weekends? +
Not during the survival swim phase. Shallow-water play and floaties can undo the survival motor patterns we're building. After your child Levels Up, I'll show you exactly how to practice safely and confidently so you can keep those skills strong.
What happens after my child Levels Up? +
After Level Up, your swimmer can continue with: maintenance lessons, refresher sessions, one-on-one 30-minute learn-to-swim, group lessons (for ages 3 to 5 graduates or 5+), Parent & Me (ages 3 and under), or adult lessons. There's always a next step. We grow with your family.
How do payments work? +
Survival Swim is billed weekly. Private, mobile, and adult lessons can be paid as single lessons, prepaid packages, or a monthly membership. Memberships require a monthly commitment and a 7-day cancellation notice. I accept Stripe (all major credit and debit cards) or cash. A one-time, non-refundable $100 registration fee applies to all programs.
What's the difference between your programs? +
Survival Swim (6 months to 6 years): 15-minute private lessons, 3 days a week, taught by Becca at our pool. Builds the automatic response: roll, float, breathe, swim to safety. Most complete in 6 to 8 weeks. This is the foundation.

Private Lessons (Ages 4+): One-on-one learn-to-swim and stroke development. Technique, endurance, and water confidence built on a safety-first foundation.

Mobile Learn-to-Swim (All ages): The same lessons delivered to your home or community pool. Availability depends on location.

Small Group, Sibling & Friend (Ages 3+): Discounted per-swimmer rates for siblings, friends, homeschool, and neighborhood groups. Contact us for custom pricing.

Adult Swim Lessons (18+): Private sessions for beginner adults. We start exactly where you are.

Parent & Me (Coming Soon): A gentle, bonding introduction to water safety for our youngest swimmers.
How does the enrollment process work? +
Here's exactly how it works: 1. Reach out first: contact me to check availability and make sure AquaSquad is the right fit for your family. 2. Get confirmed: once I confirm a spot for you, I'll let you know. If I'm full, I'll add you to the waitlist and reach out when a spot opens. 3. Register and pay: complete registration and submit payment once your spot is confirmed. 4. I'll reach out: I'll contact you with your schedule, lesson plan, and everything you need to know before day one.
