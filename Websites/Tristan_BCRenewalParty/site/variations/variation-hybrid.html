Why We're Running

Politics has protected the status quo. Not people.

For too long, politics in British Columbia has been about protecting the status quo, not protecting people. Housing costs have pushed families out of their communities. Healthcare waitlists have left loved ones in pain. Wages have not kept up with the cost of living.

A lot of British Columbians feel politically homeless, tired of the same old back-and-forth that got us here. We are running because change only comes when everyday people step forward. We are not here to play politics. We are here to build solutions that work.

Leadership · Experience · Values

We're not politicians. We're builders.

The BC Renewal Party was founded by everyday people who are tired of waiting for the system to fix itself: workers, parents, small business owners, and entrepreneurs.

We believe BC can be bold again, not by clinging to old ideologies, but by investing in real solutions that help people live, work, and thrive. We are creating a genuine alternative.

Meet the Founder

Meet Tristan Tolley.

Our founder, Tristan Tolley, is a 25-year-old entrepreneur and caregiver who stepped up to raise his younger brothers after losing his father. He runs a private swim-lessons business and has his first child on the way. He has lived the failures of the current system: education cuts growing up, a healthcare system that makes families wait, and housing speculation that put homeownership out of reach for his generation.

"I never planned to start a political party. But I looked around and saw a province where young people cannot afford to live, work, or dream. If we do not step up, no one will. This movement is not about power. It is about possibility, for every person who has been overlooked, overtaxed, or underestimated."
Tristan Tolley
Founder, BC Renewal Party
