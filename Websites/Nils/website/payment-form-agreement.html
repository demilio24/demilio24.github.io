1. Ownership of Work

You own only what you paid a setup or development fee for. Everything else we build, we own, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

This covers websites, funnels, code, automations, workflows, integrations, ad creative, and every other deliverable we produce. Work built under your membership that you were not separately charged to create is licensed to you only while your membership is active: you are renting access, not buying it. If you pause or cancel, that access ends and we may take the membership work offline.

When a deliverable carries a setup or development fee, we tell you before you buy, and you own that deliverable and keep it even if you cancel. A website is the common case: you own the website itself, but extras built around it like forms, workflows, and automations still need an active membership to keep running. You are welcome to take code you own and modify or re-host it elsewhere, but that is on you, as we do not offer a service to move or re-host your project.

We never claim your own brand, logos, content, customer data, domains, or accounts. Those remain yours at all times.

4. All Sales Final

All payments are final. No refunds. No chargebacks. By submitting payment, you agree not to dispute charges through your bank or payment processor. If there is a billing issue, contact us and we will sort it out.

5. Platform Usage Costs

You are responsible for the platform usage on your account: emails, SMS, phone calls, AI features, and extra usage like webhooks or premium actions. These are billed at exact provider cost with zero markup, the same pricing our provider offers us.

Every account starts with a $0 balance. Each email or SMS you send, or extra usage you trigger, costs a fraction of a cent and draws down that balance. Once it goes negative, we recharge the account using your payment method on file to keep your services running. For example, once you start using the services we recharge $10, and that amount stays on the account as credit until it is used.

Please note: these payments go directly to our providers even though our name appears on the transaction (we keep none of it), and all recharges are non-refundable.
