Meet The Team

The Instructors Behind Every Belt.

A handpicked team of certified Systema and Karate instructors, led by founder Tom Floyd. Every coach trains directly under Tom and lives the Systema standard.

Tom Floyd · Founder

Tom Floyd

Founder · Certified Systema Instructor

Tom is a lifelong martial artist who specializes in working with children and adults to build confidence, physical ability, and overall well-being. His sincerity and talent for teaching complex, instinct-based self-defense to students from all walks of life set him apart.

He began training at age five, and holds a third-degree black belt in Ryukyu Kempo, a second-degree black belt in Shorin-Ryu, and an honorary first-degree in Goju Ryu. After discovering Systema under Dave Merrill, the first certified Systema instructor in the U.S., Tom earned his certification under Vladimir Vasiliev in Toronto, and has had the privilege of training with Mikhail Ryabko, founder of Ryabko Systema.

Nearly three decades later, his approach has gained national recognition. Instructors from around the world study Tom's model for teaching children in schools and camps. His Active School Shooter: Teacher and Student Safety program has received extensive local news coverage, and he is a featured instructor at Systema seminars across North America and Mexico.
